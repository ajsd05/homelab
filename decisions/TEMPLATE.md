# NNNN — Short title in plain language

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Reversed

## Context

What situation forced a choice. What constraints were in play — hardware, time, money,
knowledge. Enough that someone reading in a year understands the pressure without needing
to remember it.

## Options considered

**Option A** — what it is, why it appealed, what it costs.

**Option B** — same.

Two or three is usually right. If there was only ever one option, this probably isn't a
decision worth recording.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What this makes easy. What it makes hard. What it rules out.

Be honest about the downside — a decision record with no costs listed reads as unconsidered.

## Revisit when

The condition that would make this choice wrong. "When there's a second disk." "If RAM
goes above 32 GB." "If more than three people use it."

Not every decision has one. Most do.
